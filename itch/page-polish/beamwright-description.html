Bounce, bend, and split light.

Mount mirrors, lenses, and prisms on an optical bench. Build a path from the source to every photocell, and watch your solution come to life.

Play in your browser. Headphones recommended.

The light is the drum machine.

Each source fires a pulse. Every mirror, lens, and photocell it meets becomes a drum hit. Change the light path, change the beat.

Start simple. Get wonderfully tangled.

  • 22 puzzles: learn the basics, then experiment with fibers, raindrops, diamonds, and a hot-air mirage.
  • Free bench: try every part with unlimited pieces.
  • Level builder: design a bench, check it with the solver, and share it as a code.
  • Community benches: play puzzles made by other players.
  • Make it readable: choose Brushed Steel, Amber Lab, Ocean, or High Contrast, and adjust the glow.

How to play

Drag a piece from the rack onto the bench. Tap or click it to select it, then tap again to rotate it. Guide light into every photocell.

  • Turn a selected piece: tap again, press Q / E, or use the mouse wheel.
  • Put a piece back: drag it off the bench.
  • Menu: use the menu button or press Esc.

Build something worth sharing.

In the bench maker, choose check to test your puzzle. Share its BW1- code in the community below. To play a shared puzzle, open the bench picker and choose Open a code.
